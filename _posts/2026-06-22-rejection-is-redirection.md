For the last three years I am working as a consultant on AI, BI and engineering topics. Coming from a background in AI and Computational Science, my job fits my background. However, I still sometimes feel like something is missing.

When looking around for other job opportunities, I find it difficult to pinpoint what it is I am looking for. Some time ago I was applying on an Engineering role where I was able to go through all three interview rounds. I ended up not getting an offer, but this result  actually feld like a relief. 

It reminded me of a very knowledgeable person once telling me that ***"Rejection is redirection"***. 

I think you can take this in two ways. At first you could say this an easy way out when you have not succeeded, perhaps it can even work demotivating and makes you give up more easily. But I think you should read it more as a "follow your gut" instinct. I took the interviews very serious, but noticed during the rounds that what the company is focussing on was maybe not aligned with my personal vision.

Here I am learning that looking for a job should be a match on two sides to make it a good fit. This is a luxury position, since not everyone is able to be picky when it comes to work. But I do think it is something that is worth giving some time to think about.
